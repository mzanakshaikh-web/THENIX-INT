05 — IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Clear terms.
Better coaching.

Please review the coaching and payment policies before registering.

01

Payment

  • Monthly packages are paid upfront.
  • Monthly subscriptions cover four weeks of coaching.
  • Individual services require payment before delivery.
  • Payments can be made in USD, AED or ZMW through mutually agreed payment methods.
  • Local and international prices are separate pricing structures.
02

Communication

  • WhatsApp or another mutually agreed communication platform.
  • Normal feedback is provided within 24–48 hours.
  • Coaching days and submission schedules are agreed upon before training begins.
03

Training requirements

  • Submit clear exercise videos when requested.
  • Follow assigned training schedules.
  • Disclose relevant injuries or physical limitations before training.
  • Stop and report exercises that cause pain.
04

Important notes

  • Programmes are adapted to individual ability and available equipment.
  • Nutrition and personalised dietary planning are not included.
  • Results depend on consistency, effort, recovery and other personal factors.
  • Coaching is educational and fitness-oriented, not a substitute for medical or physiotherapy care.
